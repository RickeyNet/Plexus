# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in Plexus regardless of background or experience. This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project discussions, and to interactions when representing the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and give others room to ask questions and learn.
- Critique the work, not the person. Give specific, constructive feedback and consider differing viewpoints.
- Respect people's privacy and share their information only with permission.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing someone's private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to a project maintainer using any contact they list on their GitHub profile. If that isn't possible or the report involves a maintainer, you can [report abuse to GitHub](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). Include relevant links, dates, and enough context to understand what happened. Do not post personal information or sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers will handle reports as discreetly as possible and share information only as needed to respond. Depending on the severity and history, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban an account from project spaces. Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
